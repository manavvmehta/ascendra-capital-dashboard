YOU DO NOT NEED TO OPEN ANYTHING IN HERE.

These are Claude's working parts. They are kept in the open rather
than hidden away so that nothing about the testing is a black box -
if you ever want to know exactly what a rule does, it is written
down here in plain terms.


THE FILES
---------

inv_hs.py
    YOUR DETECTOR, COPIED EXACTLY.

    This is the pattern-spotting code from your live scanner,
    character for character. It has deliberately NOT been tidied,
    improved or corrected.

    That is the whole point. It is the thing being tested. If it
    were quietly improved first, the results would describe some
    other scanner rather than yours.

    Any change we decide to make later gets added as a separate,
    clearly labelled version, so we can measure what that one
    change did on its own.


replay.py
    THE TRADE RULES.

    Turns a spotted pattern into a paper trade: buy at the next
    day's open, stop at the right shoulder low, sell half at the
    first target and move the stop up to breakeven, sell the rest
    at the second target.

    Two rules in here matter more than they look:

    - If a single day touches both your stop and your target, it
      is recorded as a LOSS. A daily bar does not tell you which
      came first, and assuming the good one would make every
      result look better than it really was.

    - The original stop is stored separately and never overwritten,
      even after the stop moves up to breakeven. Otherwise every
      winning trade would quietly measure its risk against the
      wrong number, and the mistake would be invisible.


selftest.py
    THE PROOF IT WORKS.

    Rebuilds your STLTECH trade from scratch and checks every
    number against your own document:

        entry     Rs 700.00         stop      Rs 601.00
        target 1  Rs 766.00         target 2  Rs 874.75
        left-shoulder alternative target  Rs 813.75
        stop 14.1% below entry, position 285 shares
        breakout on 1.09x average volume

    It also tests all four ways a trade can end, and the awkward
    case of a day that touches both stop and target.
