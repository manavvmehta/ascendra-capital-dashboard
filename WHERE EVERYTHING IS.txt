================================================================
   ASCENDRA CAPITAL — what is in each folder
================================================================


   YOU ONLY EVER OPEN ONE FILE
   ---------------------------

       5 LIVE FEED \ ASCENDRA.py

   Everything is behind that one menu:

       1   Log in to Zerodha        every morning, first
       2   Start the desk           the engine + your dashboard
       3   Scan now                 after the close
       4   Backtest                 three years, one-off
       5   Get intraday data        for the F&O study — one-off,
                                    ~5 minutes, after the close

   Nothing else in any folder needs to be opened by hand.


   THE FOLDERS
   -----------

   5 LIVE FEED     The programs. ASCENDRA.py and everything it runs.
                   This is the only folder with anything you open.

   WEBSITE         Your dashboard page. Opens by itself at
                   localhost:5055 when the desk is running.

   DATA            THE TRADING RECORD. Every call ever made lives in
                   calls_log.csv here, permanently. The engine's own
                   working files sit alongside it, all starting with a
                   dot. Do not delete anything in here — this folder is
                   your history and it cannot be rebuilt.

   NSE FO BACKTEST  THE F&O STUDY. Downloaded price history, indicator
                   tests, the futures strategy — everything to do with
                   the F&O work lives here and nowhere else. Nothing in
                   here affects live trading, and anything in here can be
                   deleted and downloaded again.
                   Jenil's five setups are NOT touched by any of it.

   3 CALL ENGINE   The rules that find the trades. Do not edit by hand.

   4 REFERENCE     Original documents, kept for reference only.

   1 DOWNLOAD DATA  Old work, no longer used. Kept, not running.
   2 ENGINE         The head-and-shoulders scanner, parked.


   THE TWO THAT MATTER
   -------------------

       DATA             is your record.     Never delete.
       NSE FO BACKTEST  is your workbench.  Safe to delete.


================================================================
   THE MORNING ROUTINE

   Open ASCENDRA.py, press 1, press 2, leave the black window open.
   That is all. See "MORNING - what to do.txt" if anything looks
   wrong.
