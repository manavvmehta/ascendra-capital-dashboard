================================================================
  1  DOWNLOAD DATA
================================================================

WHAT TO DO
    Double-click:   DOWNLOAD DATA - double click me.py

    A black window opens and starts working. Leave it alone for
    about an hour.


WHAT YOU WILL SEE
    Step 1   the list of companies          about 1 minute
    Step 2   ten years of daily prices      about 40 minutes
    Step 3   extras                         skipped if NSE refuses
    Step 4   a check that it all arrived    about 1 minute

    During step 2 you will see dates scrolling past and a
    percentage climbing. As long as that number is going up, it is
    healthy. It is meant to look slow - there is a deliberate pause
    between requests so NSE does not cut us off.


IF YOU NEED TO STOP
    Just close the window. Nothing is lost.

    Double-click the file again whenever you like and it carries on
    from exactly where it stopped. It never downloads the same day
    twice.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
    Take a screenshot of the black window and send it to Claude.
    Do not try to fix it yourself - some of the warnings that
    appear are completely normal and only Claude will know which.


WHAT APPEARS IN HERE
    A folder called "nse_data" containing:

        master\        the list of every NSE company
        bhav\          one file per trading day, ten years of them
        corpactions\   stock splits and bonus issues, if available

    Do not rename or reorganise anything inside nse_data. The
    engine looks for those exact names.
