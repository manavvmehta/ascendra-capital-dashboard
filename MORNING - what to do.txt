================================================================
   EVERY TRADING MORNING          keep this open until it sticks
================================================================


   BEFORE 9:15
   -----------

   1.  Double-click        ASCENDRA.py
       (in 5 LIVE FEED)

   2.  Press  1            log in to Zerodha

          - your browser opens the Zerodha login
          - log in as normal
          - it lands on a page that FAILS TO LOAD. That is
            CORRECT. Nothing has gone wrong.
          - copy the WHOLE address from the address bar
          - paste it into the black window, press Enter
          - it shows live prices. That is the feed working.

   3.  Press Enter         back to the menu

   4.  Press  2            start the engine

          - your dashboard opens at localhost:5055

   5.  LEAVE THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN.

       That window IS the engine. Close it and open calls stop
       being watched - and a stop nobody is watching is not a
       stop.


   THEN NOTHING
   ------------

   The engine handles the rest:

       every 150 seconds    scans the market for new setups
       every 5 seconds      re-checks every open call
       every 10 minutes     sweeps all 2,300 for swing setups
       15:10                closes intraday positions
       15:45                runs the closing swing scan
       all day              writes everything to DATA\calls_log.csv

   Look at the dashboard whenever you like: localhost:5055


   AT THE END OF THE DAY
   ---------------------

   Close the window. Everything is already saved.


================================================================
   IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
================================================================

   "Today's login is missing"
        You pressed 2 before 1. Press 1 first.

   Dashboard will not load
        The black window is closed. Start it again.

   No calls appearing
        Normal for a quiet morning. The engine is selective -
        it only fires when a setup genuinely appears. Check the
        WATCHING tab: that shows ideas it built and declined,
        so you can tell "nothing happening" from "nothing
        firing".

   Anything else
        Screenshot the black window and send it to Claude.
        Do not try to fix it - several of the warnings that
        appear are completely normal, and only the full text
        says which.


================================================================
   THE ONE THING TO REMEMBER

   Set your PC never to sleep on trading days. If it sleeps
   mid-session the engine goes quiet without saying so, and
   you lose the day's record.
================================================================
