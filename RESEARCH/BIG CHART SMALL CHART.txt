================================================================================
   PATTERN ON THE BIG CHART, ENTRY ON THE SMALL ONE
   Tested 11/09/2026 for ASCENDRA CAPITAL
================================================================================

MM was right that this had never been tested. Both earlier runs found the
pattern and took the trade on the SAME chart. That throws away the one real
mechanical advantage the method has, and he said so:

   "we have to see the pattern on bigger time frame and entry and exit in
    smaller time frame ... then we can make money for sure"

WHAT THE COMBINATION DOES

   target   from the BIG chart - the measured move, unchanged
   stop     from the SMALL chart - the low of the little pullback, instead of
            the far side of the whole pattern

THE ENTRY RULE, fixed before the test

   1. the pattern completes on the big chart - a bar CLOSES beyond the trigger
   2. drop to the small chart and WAIT. Nothing is bought while price runs.
   3. price must pull back toward the level it just broke
   4. entry: the first small bar that CLOSES beyond the previous small bar's
      high (its low for a short), filled at the NEXT bar's open
   5. stop: the lowest low of that pullback
   6. target: the big chart's measured move, unchanged
   7. no entry within three big bars = MISSED, and counted as a miss

Costs follow what the trade did: same day 0.082%, held overnight 0.222% for a
long and 0.07% for a short in futures - shorts overnight only in the 216
stocks that have them. Rs 5 crore a day liquidity throughout.

Every single combination is a large improvement on taking the same pattern on

and the loss shrinks by 90%. The idea is mechanically sound and it does what
he said it would do.

It does not cross zero. Not one of the five.


================================================================================
   THE STOP — the one knob worth turning, and it does not save it
================================================================================

At a 1.1% stop against a measured move roughly ten times that, breakeven needs
a 10% win rate. The system wins 10.5%. That is not a strategy, it is a coin
balanced on its edge - a few paise of slippage a trade decides it either way.

So the stop was widened. Four settings, declared in advance, judged on both
halves. Twenty cells in total:

   DAILY > 15-MIN     1.0x -105   1.5x  -97   2.0x -145   3.0x  -88
   DAILY > HOURLY     1.0x  -72   1.5x -180   2.0x -241   3.0x -199
   75-MIN > 15-MIN    1.0x -140   1.5x -145   2.0x -150   3.0x -122

All twenty lose. The best cell in the whole study is DAILY pattern with an
HOURLY entry at the pullback low: -Rs 72 a trade.


================================================================================
   THE WARNING THAT MATTERS MORE THAN THE AVERAGE
================================================================================

Every daily-based combination is heavily NEGATIVE in the first half of the
year and POSITIVE in the second:

   DAILY > 15-MIN      1st half  -Rs 400      2nd half  +Rs 190
   DAILY > HOURLY      1st half  -Rs 569      2nd half  +Rs 423
   DAILY > 15-MIN 3x   1st half  -Rs 926      2nd half  +Rs 737

That is the calendar talking, not an edge. Which half of the year you test
decides the answer, and a rule whose sign depends on the period is not a rule.
The one combination that IS stable across both halves - 75-minute patterns
with 15-minute entries, -149 and -131 - is stably negative.

If the second half were the real number, six more months of live calls would
show it. That is the only honest way to settle it, and it costs nothing but
patience: the calls can be recorded without a rupee being deployed, exactly
the way the SMC tab already works.


================================================================================
   WHAT I WOULD NOT DO NEXT
================================================================================

Turn a third knob. Five combinations x four stops is twenty cells already, on
top of the sixteen patterns x three entries x two timeframes from this
morning. Keep slicing and something will come out positive by luck alone.

The finding stands on its own and it is not nothing: MM's method takes the
same signal from -Rs 976 to -Rs 93. The signal is still the problem, not the
execution.
