================================================================================
   RETEST vs BREAKOUT  —  and the SMC touch question
   11/09/2026,  ASCENDRA CAPITAL
================================================================================

MM asked whether the entries and exits were right, and pointed at the one
thing that was missing: every pattern had been tested on the BREAKOUT entry
only. Buying the breakout is buying the worst price in the move, and switching
to the pullback is exactly what turned the first SMC test from losing to
making money before costs. So it deserved testing.

Three entries, all sixteen patterns, both timeframes. Same stops, same
targets, nothing else changed:

   BREAK    the next bar's open after the confirming close
   RETEST   a limit back AT the broken level, filled only if a later bar
            actually trades there within 10 bars
   HALF     a limit half way between the two

An unfilled limit is counted as a MISS, not quietly dropped - otherwise
"waiting for the pullback" just deletes the losers.

   RESULT, per trade at Rs 1,00,000, after costs

                       BREAK      RETEST        HALF
      daily             -467        -445        -370
      15-minute          -46         -59         -55

It does not fix them. On daily the retest is 22 rupees better than the
breakout and still loses; the half-way entry is the best of the three and
still loses. On 15-minute bars the pullback is WORSE, because one trade in
four never comes back and those are disproportionately the ones that ran.

Two cells did turn positive on daily - bull flag (+193 retest, +221 half,
from -143) and cup and handle (+701 on the half entry). They are 2 cells out
of 96 tried, and both were already shown to lose to a same-day coin toss.
They are not a finding, they are what 96 tries looks like.

The full tables are in retest.txt.


================================================================================
   SMC — WHY THERE ARE NO CALLS, AND WHAT I CANNOT YET TELL YOU
================================================================================

The cause is settled and is not a bug: the 3-touch rule, raised from 2 on
09/09, cut the CHoCH scan from 8-18 signals a day to 0-4. Replayed on real
bars: 08/09 eight down to two, 09/09 eighteen down to four, 10/09 four down
to zero. The "completed hours only" change costs almost nothing.

WHAT I TRIED, AND WHY I AM NOT REPORTING THE NUMBER

I replayed the CHoCH scan over a year at 2, 3 and 4 touches. It came back
claiming a 93% win rate and Rs 1,246 a trade. That is not a result, it is a
bug, and I checked it before telling you rather than after.

The check: take the 504 real settled SMC calls out of the desk's own log and
re-settle them with the backtest's own rules from the same candles. If the
backtest is faithful it should reproduce what actually happened.

      outcome        the record      my replay
      target hit            129             40
      stopped               223            287
      timed out              48              0
      never filled          104            177
      win rate            32.2%          12.2%

It agrees with the record on 54% of calls. It is not faithful, so nothing it
says about 2 versus 3 touches means anything. Two faults found: the replay
settles on HOUR bars, so the 15:10 square-off never fires (hour bars are
stamped 14:15, never 15:10) - which is where the 48 missing time exits went;
and the desk settles live calls across repeated intraday passes that a single
end-of-day replay cannot reproduce.

WHY YOUR OWN RECORD CANNOT ANSWER IT EITHER

Of 553 live intraday SMC calls, 552 came from the OLD sweep detector. The
CHoCH detector went live on 09/09 and has produced exactly ONE call. There is
no live evidence on touch counts because there are no live CHoCH trades.

THE SOLUTION I WOULD PUT TO YOU

Put it back to 2 - not because 2 is better, but because at 3 the desk makes
zero calls on a normal day, and a setting that produces no trades can never be
tested. You would be stuck guessing for ever.

Every CHoCH call already stamps its own touch count in the record ("3x level"
in the swept column). So run at 2, let the calls accumulate for a few weeks,
and then your OWN trades answer whether a level that held three times is worth
more than one that held twice. That is the same rule we agreed on 03/09 for
the score gate: do not tighten a filter on a backtest when a few weeks of real
calls will settle it properly.

Nothing has been changed. The desk is still at 3 touches.
