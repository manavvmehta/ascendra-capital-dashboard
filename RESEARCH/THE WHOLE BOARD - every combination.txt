================================================================================
   HOW IT WAS JUDGED, AND WHY THIS PART IS THE WHOLE POINT
================================================================================

At 31,000 tests, roughly 1,550 will look good at p<0.05 from luck alone. Any
"best combination" picked out of a table that size is meaningless. So the
sweep was run one way only:

    CHOOSE on the FIRST half of the data.
    JUDGE on the SECOND half, which the choosing never saw.

If the winners are real, far more than half of them should win again in the
second half. If they are noise, about half will - that is what a coin does.

Read the last two columns. On every single timeframe, FEWER combinations
survived than luck alone would have produced.

That is not a near miss. It means a combination that made money in the first
half was LESS likely than a coin toss to make money in the second. Picking the
winners from a backtest was actively worse than picking at random.

This is the cleanest answer the project has produced, and it covers everything
MM asked for in one test: all the candles, all the levels, all the indicators,
every timeframe. None of it holds up.


================================================================================
   WHAT THE SURVIVORS ACTUALLY ARE — and it is not what we were looking for
================================================================================

The rows that did survive are mostly not reversal setups at all:

   hourly     bullish engulfing, bullish belt hold, bullish marubozu,
              three outside up, three white soldiers
   30-minute  the same list
   15-minute  bullish marubozu, belt hold, three outside up, three white
              soldiers

Forty-odd percent of every survivor list is a BUYING-STRENGTH candle - a big
green bar, a run of green bars - and almost all of them are long. Those are
not "price reversed at a level". They are momentum, in a year the market rose.

Which lands in the same place as the only other thing that has ever tested
positive here: momentum, where the top tenth of twelve-month winners beat the
market by Rs 385 a month. The chart is not finding turns. Where it appears to
find anything, it is finding strength - and there is a far simpler and better
tested way to buy strength than waiting for a marubozu at a pivot.


================================================================================
   AND THE CONFLUENCE RESULT FROM EARLIER
================================================================================

The Bollinger + reversal candle + RSI row that came out at +Rs 135 was one of
the 1,253 hourly combinations that looked good on the first half, and one of
the 347 that also looked good on the second. Out of 1,253, about 626 were
going to do that by chance. It was not special; it was one of the expected
ones - and its own control test said the same thing separately: it beat a
random BAR but not a random STOCK on the same hour.
