================================================================================
   LEVEL + CANDLE + INDICATOR  —  DOES CLUBBING THEM HELP?
   15/09/2026,  ASCENDRA CAPITAL
================================================================================

MM: "when the price reaches a certain level that is when indicators,
candlesticks and price action should all be clubbed ... if there is a reversal
candle at the top or bottom of the chart and indicator also shows an overbought
zone or oversold zone, thats when you place a trade. we have been backtesting
it in a wrong way till date."

He was right that nothing here had tested that. Every earlier test took ONE
thing and traded it alone.

THE THREE LEGS, built from the published rules

  1. LOCATION, four kinds, each tested separately
       ZONE   a band price has already been rejected from at least TWICE,
              drawn from the extreme wick to the cluster of bodies
       RANGE  the top or bottom 15% of the last 40 bars
       EMA    the 20, 50 or 200 EMA, within a quarter of an ATR
       BB     outside the 20,2 Bollinger band

  2. INDICATOR   stretched AND turning back. The trigger is the CROSS BACK -
                 RSI closing back above 30, not merely sitting under it. This
                 is the part most descriptions get wrong.

  3. CANDLE      hammer / shooting star, engulfing, or a doji at the extreme.

  STOP    beyond the far edge of the level, never tighter than the ATR band
          for that timeframe (1.25x on 15-min, 1.5x hourly, 2x daily), and
          never in open space - it must sit beyond a real swing point.
  TARGET  twice the risk.
  ENTRY   the next bar's open after the signal bar closes.

THE INDICATOR MATHS WAS CHECKED, not assumed: our RSI reproduces Wilder's own
published example to two decimal places.

AND THE POINT: every leg is also tested ALONE and in every PAIR, on the same
bars, so the question is answered directly - is the edge in the AGREEMENT?


================================================================================
   THE ANSWER: ON THE HOURLY CHART, YES
================================================================================

Every leg on its own loses. Every pair is mixed. All three together is
POSITIVE on every kind of level:

      ALL THREE, Bollinger        +Rs 135 a trade    2,693 trades
      ALL THREE, range extreme     +Rs 52            2,763
      ALL THREE, EMA               +Rs 23               89
      ALL THREE, zone              +Rs 19            4,152

The Bollinger one is positive in BOTH halves of the year (+162 then +110) -
the first thing in this whole project to do that.

On 15-MINUTE the same ORDERING holds - all three (-Rs 26) beats the pairs
(-Rs 50) beats candle alone (-Rs 71) - but nothing crosses zero. At that speed
the cost eats it.

On DAILY everything loses badly. Delivery costs 0.235% a round trip and a 2x
ATR stop on a daily bar is a wide stop; the two together are too much.

NOT YET PROVEN. 19 rows on each of three timeframes is 57 cells, and with that
many something can look good by luck. The control test - the same stock, same
day, same holding time, random entry - has not been run on these yet. Until it
has, this is a promising signal and not a result.
