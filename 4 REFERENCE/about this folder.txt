  4  REFERENCE

Background material. Nothing here runs - it is the written record
of what the rules are and what we are trying to find out.


WHAT BELONGS IN HERE
--------------------

The original rules document
    The handoff describing the pattern, the detector, the trade
    rules, the stock universe, and the open questions. This is the
    source of truth for what the scanner is supposed to do.

Anything else worth keeping
    Notes, screenshots of charts you want looked at, descriptions
    of other dashboard tabs, rules for other patterns.

    If you want Claude to know about something, put it here and
    say so. Nothing in this folder is read automatically.


THE OPEN QUESTIONS, IN SHORT
----------------------------

Every one of these is currently a guess in the live code. Something
had to be chosen, so something was. These are what the backtest is
for:

    Which shoulder sets the first target
    Whether the neckline must be horizontal
    How unequal the two shoulders may be
    Whether the two sides must be similar in TIME, not just price
    Whether the head must be the lowest point in the window
    How fresh a breakout must be to still be worth taking
    What kills a pattern that is still forming
    Whether to wait for a pullback to the neckline before entering
    Where the stop goes - the biggest lever of all
    Whether volume should be a requirement again
    How big a swing counts as a shoulder at all
    Anything being missed entirely - trend, sector, the wider market


AND ONE MORE, FOUND SINCE
-------------------------

    Whether the scanner should report the pattern that BROKE OUT
    rather than the best-looking one it can find.

    See "READ ME FIRST" for what this means in practice. It was
    not on the original list and it may matter more than several
    that were.
