ON WHAT BASIS THE EQUITY CALLS ARE ISSUED
A live intraday + swing scanner on NSE cash equities
Generated 01 September 2026 from the running code
================================================================================

Everything below is read out of the live engine at the moment this file was
written. If a threshold here disagrees with the code, the file is stale - it is
regenerated by re-running its script.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1  ·  THE FUNNEL, IN ORDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every 150 seconds, during market hours:

  1. QUOTE THE WHOLE EQUITY UNIVERSE
     A snapshot of every NSE equity the engine tracks.

  2. TRADABILITY GATE  (section 2)
     Anything that fails is dropped before it can become a call.

  3. STAGE-1 SHORTLIST  (section 3)
     A cheap score from the snapshot alone ranks everything, and only the top
     25 go further. Fetching 15-minute candles for the whole board
     every cycle is not affordable; this is the filter that makes the rest
     possible.

  4. 15-MINUTE CANDLES for those 25 names only.

  5. FIVE SETUPS run against those candles  (section 4)
     Each is independent. A stock can match more than one.

  6. CONFLUENCE SCORE  (section 5)
     Score >= 6.5 becomes a CALL. Below that it is recorded as a
     'watching' row and never shown as actionable.

  7. LIFECYCLE  (section 6)
     A call waits for its entry, then runs to a target, a stop, or a time exit.

Open calls are re-checked every 5 seconds, separately from the
150-second hunt. That split exists because a stop that is only
checked every 150 seconds is not a stop.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2  ·  THE TRADABILITY GATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Applied to every equity, every cycle, immediately before a call becomes real.

    minimum price            Rs20
    minimum 20-day turnover  Rs5 crore per day
    minimum daily history    enough candles to compute ATR and averages
    must actually move       a stock with no ATR cannot pay for its own spread

Indices are exempt - an index has no turnover, and applying an equity floor to
one would silence it.

This gate is re-tested for each symbol at the moment the call would be issued,
not once when the universe is built. A universe list can be stale by the time
a signal fires.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3  ·  THE STAGE-1 SHORTLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ranked on a single cheap number per stock, computed from the day's snapshot:

    rvol x 2                relative volume, time-adjusted for how much of the
                            session has elapsed so a 9:30 reading is not
                            compared against a full day
    + range / ATR14         how far today has travelled in ATR terms
    + |% change| x 0.3
    + edge                  how close the price sits to the day's high or low,
                            whichever is nearer - a stock pinned at one
                            extreme is more interesting than one mid-range

Top 25 by that number get 15-minute candles. Nothing else does.

This is a COARSE filter and it is the least examined part of the engine. A good
setup on a stock ranked 26th is never seen. See the open questions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4  ·  THE FIVE SETUPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"Participation" below means the signal bar's volume against the 20-bar average,
or its range against the 20-bar average range when volume is unusable. Some
setups treat it as a HARD GATE - fail it and the setup returns nothing at all.

4.1  PULLBACK                                 [intraday]
     With the daily trend. Price pulls back to VWAP / EMA20, RSI resets and
     turns, and the turn bar shows participation.
     participation  >= 1.1x (volume) or 1.2x (range) - SOFT, scores but does not gate
     entry          just beyond the turn bar's extreme (+0.05 ATR)
     stop           the turn bar's low, or 1.2 ATR, whichever is further
     targets        T1 = 1R, T2 = 2R

4.2  ORB - opening range breakout             [intraday]
     The 9:15-9:45 range, broken with trend, between 09:45 and 11:30 only.
     participation  >= 1.3x (volume) / 1.2x (range) - HARD GATE
     opening range  must be 0.2 to 0.9 of the daily ATR: too narrow is noise,
                    too wide means the move already happened
     entry          the breaking close
     stop           the MIDDLE of the opening range
     targets        T1 = 0.75 x range, T2 = 1.5 x range

4.3  SQUEEZE                                  [intraday]
     Bollinger bandwidth in the bottom quartile of its own recent history, then
     a close outside the band. With-trend only.
     participation  >= 1.5x (volume) / 1.35x (range) - HARD GATE
     entry          the breaking close
     stop           the band midline, or 1.2 ATR if that is too far
     targets        T1 = 1R, T2 = 2R

4.4  BREAKOUT-20D                             [swing, 3-10 sessions]
     A fresh 20-day Donchian high (or low), broken today, no more than 1.5%
     through it - the move must still be catchable.
     participation  >= 2.5x DAILY volume - HARD GATE
                    Live record 21/07-10/08: breakouts under 2.5x went 0W/7SL.
                    A breakout without volume is a trap.
     rejected       if daily RSI > 78 - an already-vertical chart
     entry          the current price
     stop           2 x daily ATR, or just under the broken level, whichever
                    is tighter
     targets        T1 = 2R, T2 = 3.5R
     bonus          within 5% of the 52-week high (George & Hwang: the
                    strongest momentum zone)

4.5  VWAP-FLIP                                [intraday]
     Price crosses VWAP and holds the other side.
     participation  >= 1.2x - HARD GATE
     entry          the flip close
     stop           0.8 ATR the far side of VWAP
     targets        T1 = 1R, T2 = 2R

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5  ·  THE CONFLUENCE SCORE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each setup starts at a base and adds points for confirming conditions. The
score is capped at 10. 6.5 or more fires; below that the row is
recorded as 'watching' and is not actionable. 8.0+ is labelled HIGH
conviction.

    setup           base   what can be added
    ------------------------------------------------------------------------
    PULLBACK        5.0    ADX +1.0 · participation +1.0 · right side of VWAP
                           +0.5 · order-flow agreement +1.0 · room to target
                           +0.5
    ORB             5.5    ADX +1.0 · gap alignment +0.5
    SQUEEZE         5.5    break aligns with the daily trend +1.5
    BREAKOUT-20D    5.5    participation +1.0 (>=1.5x) or +0.5 (>=1.2x) ·
                           within 5% of 52w high +1.5 · ADX >= 22 +1.0
    VWAP-FLIP       5.0    participation +1.0 · trend +1.0 · flow +1.5

Every published call records its components separately, so any score can be
decomposed after the fact rather than reverse-engineered from a total.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6  ·  WHAT HAPPENS AFTER A CALL IS ISSUED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    WAITING     the entry has not been reached yet
    LIVE        the entry was reached
    T1_HIT      first target reached
    T2_HIT      second target reached - the trade is closed
    SL_HIT      stopped
    TIME_EXIT   intraday calls are closed at 15:10 regardless
    EXPIRED     the entry was never reached and the call was withdrawn

Rules that matter:

  · Entry direction depends on which side of the entry the market was on WHEN
    THE CALL WAS ISSUED. If the market was below a long entry, the price must
    rise into it (a breakout). If it was already above, the price must FALL to
    it (a pullback into a resting limit). Testing only one direction filed a
    real, filled trade as 'never triggered' on 01/09.

  · A waiting call that trades through its STOP before ever reaching its entry
    is abandoned, not entered.

  · Un-triggered calls expire after 90 minutes.

  · Swing calls are held at most 10 sessions.

  · After a stop, that symbol and direction are locked out for the rest of the
    day. Re-firing the same idea after it just failed is how one bad read
    becomes five.

  · A call whose price stops arriving for 60 consecutive checks
    (~5 minutes) is VOIDED, not left open. A
    position displayed as live whose stop cannot fire is a safety system that
    has stopped without saying so.

READ THOSE CONFIDENCE INTERVALS BEFORE DRAWING CONCLUSIONS. Several of them

THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST, AND IT IS NEGATIVE
    The confluence score does not predict the outcome. Across the resolved
    calls the rank correlation between score and R is close to zero and not
    statistically distinguishable from none at all.

    Part of that is structural: the score buckets are not made of the same
    setups. SQUEEZE only ever publishes at one score, and the highest scores
    are almost entirely BREAKOUT-20D. So comparing a high bucket with a low
    one compares SETUPS, not conviction.

    Within a single setup, where the comparison is clean, no score effect
    survives at this sample size either.

    That is the single biggest open problem in this engine and it is where an
    analyst's judgement would be worth the most.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8  ·  WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT IN THE ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Saying what was left out is as informative as what was put in.

  · No fundamentals, no news, no earnings dates. Price and volume only.
  · No machine learning, no fitted parameters. Every threshold is a number
    someone chose and can defend; none was optimised on past returns.
  · No position sizing inside the signal. The call states a stop; size is a
    separate decision made elsewhere.
  · No shorting of cash equity for multi-day holds - it cannot be held
    overnight.
  · No averaging down, no re-entry after a stop on the same day.
  · No index or sector filter. A call fires on its own merits regardless of
    what the broad market is doing that day.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9  ·  OPEN QUESTIONS - WHERE JUDGEMENT WOULD HELP MOST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. THE SCORE DOES NOT WORK. Should it be rebuilt, re-weighted per setup, or
    abandoned in favour of a simple per-setup filter? It currently decides what
    fires, and there is no evidence it should.

Q2. THE STAGE-1 SHORTLIST is 25 names chosen by a formula nobody
    has ever validated. How much is it costing? Is rvol the right leading term?

Q3. PULLBACK is the weakest setup by mean R and it is also the one with the
    softest gates - participation only scores, never blocks. Should it gate?

Q4. BREAKOUT-20D has the widest outcome spread of any setup. Is a 2 x ATR stop
    right for a swing breakout, or should it sit under the broken level only?

Q5. NO MARKET CONTEXT. Every call is judged in isolation. Should a long be
    suppressed when the index is falling hard, or is that just curve-fitting?

Q6. TIME EXIT AT 15:10. Chosen to avoid the closing auction. Is that leaving
    money on the table, or saving it?

Q7. ORB has almost no sample because its window and range constraints are
    narrow. Are those constraints right, or are they starving a good setup?

Q8. THE PARTICIPATION THRESHOLDS differ per setup and were set by judgement.
    Are they in the right places?
