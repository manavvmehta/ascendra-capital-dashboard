================================================================
  3  CALL ENGINE
================================================================

THIS IS THE ENGINE ITSELF.

Jenil wrote it. It is the thing that produces the buy and sell
calls on his screen, and it is what will produce yours.

You do not need to open any of it.


WHY IT IS HERE RATHER THAN LEFT IN THE ZIP
------------------------------------------
Because it is the most valuable thing you have been given, and a
zip file nobody unpacks is a thing nobody knows they own.

Building a scanner like this from nothing would take weeks. You
already have it, finished and tested. What is missing is not the
brain - it is the plumbing that feeds it live prices.


THE FILES
---------

tech_calls.py
    The whole engine. Around 2,000 lines. It holds:
      - the filter that throws out stocks too cheap or too thinly
        traded to be worth a call
      - the shortlist that picks the 25 most active names each cycle
      - the five setups that look for a reason to buy or sell
      - the scoring that decides which of those become real calls
      - the lifecycle: waiting, live, target hit, stopped, expired

HOW_IT_WORKS.txt
    THE ONE TO READ IF YOU READ ANY. Plain language, no code. Every
    threshold, every rule, and - unusually honest - a section on what
    he deliberately left OUT and why.

README.md
    Shorter summary, plus his own account of the biggest unsolved
    problem in it.

OPEN_QUESTIONS.txt
    Eight things in the engine that are judgement calls rather than
    proven facts. Worth reading before trusting any of it too far.

config.py, state.py
    Small supporting files. Where things get saved, and how the
    parts talk to each other.


THE TWO TIMEFRAMES
------------------
Four of the five setups read 15-MINUTE candles and close the same
day at 15:10. They need a live price feed and cannot run without
one.

The fifth, BREAKOUT-20D, reads DAILY candles and holds for three to
ten sessions. It needs nothing but the free end-of-day data you are
already downloading.

That is why the daily one runs first. Not a compromise - it is the
only one the free data can physically support.


THE HONEST PART
---------------
His own dashboard reports live results running BELOW what his
testing predicted. He shows that on his own screen rather than
hiding it, which says something good about him and something
cautionary about the engine.

Treat it as a strong starting point that still has to prove itself
on your data. Not as a finished answer.
