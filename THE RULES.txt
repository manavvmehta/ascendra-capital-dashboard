   Stop                     set by the setup — about 0.9% of price
                            in practice
   First target  (T1)       1x the risk. HALF the position comes off
                            and the stop moves to breakeven.
   Second target (T2)       2x the risk. The rest comes off.
   Force close              15:10, every day, no exceptions
   Unfilled orders          expire 90 minutes after they are named

   Coverage each cycle      60 most active + 200 rotating, every
                            150 seconds — the whole market is seen
                            about every 25 minutes

   5.  THE F&O STUDY               (not run yet)
================================================================

       Jenil's five setups are NOT to be changed, re-tuned or
       re-tested as part of this study. tech_calls.py has never
       been edited and stays that way. If his setup is ever to be
       backtested, that is a SEPARATE job, run separately, only
       when MM asks for it.

   Question                 does one trend + one momentum + one
                            volume indicator, taken at a support or
                            resistance level, make money intraday?

   Entries                  BOUNCE off the level, not a break
                            through it
   Levels, fixed in advance previous day's high and low
                            floor pivots — P, R1, R2, S1, S2
                            opening range high and low
   Stop                     1.5 x ATR(14), clamped to 1%-2% of
                            the entry price
   Target                   2 x the stop, so winners land at 2%-4%
   Exit                     15:10

   Candidates, 4 per family
       trend      EMA20/50 · ADX-DI · Supertrend · MACD
       momentum   RSI · Stochastic · ROC · CCI
       volume     volume vs average · OBV slope · VWAP side · MFI

   64 combinations in total.

   How we avoid fooling ourselves:
       the best combination is chosen on the FIRST HALF of the
       data only, then run on the second half it has never seen;
       a permutation test compares it with random signals; all 64
       are reported, not just the winner. If the winner does not
       beat random out of sample, the answer is "nothing found"
       and we stop rather than adjusting until it looks good.

   6.  COSTS                       (NOT on the dashboard)
================================================================

   MM calculates costs himself. Every figure on the dashboard is
   GROSS — before brokerage, STT, GST and stamp duty.

   The rate card, if you want to check your own arithmetic:

       EQUITY INTRADAY, round trip on Rs 1,00,000
           brokerage      Rs 40.00   Rs 20 or 0.03%, lower of the
                                     two, each side
           STT            Rs 25.00   0.025%, SELL side only
           exchange        Rs 6.44   0.00322%, both sides
           SEBI            Rs 0.20   Rs 10 per crore
           stamp duty      Rs 3.00   0.003%, BUY side only
           GST             Rs 8.40   18% on brokerage + exchange
                                     + SEBI
           ------------------------
           TOTAL          Rs 83.04 = 0.083% of the trade

       STOCK FUTURES INTRADAY, same round trip
           TOTAL          Rs 67.37 = 0.0674%
           (cheaper — STT is half the rate and exchange charges
            are lower)

   Brokerage is effectively FIXED at about Rs 20 a side, not a
   percentage, so a bigger position costs less per rupee.

   Measured on the first two live days: costs would have taken
   HALF the gross profit — Rs 29,316 gross, Rs 14,619 net.

   Not included anywhere: SLIPPAGE, the gap between the price you
   wanted and the price you got. On thin stocks that can be larger
   than every charge above put together.

================================================================
   10.  THE SMC TAB  -  liquidity sweeps
================================================================

   WHAT IT IS
       A separate book, on purpose. NO MONEY is assigned to it.
       It takes no slot out of the Rs 50 lakh, changes no P&L
       tile, and never touches the equity book. It exists so you
       can RECORD these trades and let your own live data judge
       them.

   THE RULE, fixed in advance
       A swing point is a bar whose low is the lowest of the
       three bars either side. It is only KNOWN three bars later,
       and it is never used before that.

       A sweep is a bar that trades THROUGH that level - where
       the stops are sitting - and CLOSES BACK on the other side.
       The break happened and was rejected inside one bar.

       The sweep bar must also be a reversal candle, from your
       own documents: hammer, bullish engulfing or piercing for a
       long; shooting star, bearish engulfing or dark cloud cover
       for a short.

       Buy      a break of the sweep bar's high  (sell: its low)
       Stop     the other end of the sweep bar
       Target   twice the risk
       Out      15:10 same day on INTRADAY; on POSITIONAL it runs.

   TWO SUB-TABS
       INTRADAY     15-minute candles, 350 stocks scanned a few
                    minutes after every 15-minute candle closes.
                    Every stock that HAS A FUTURES CONTRACT goes
                    in first; the rest of the list is the most
                    liquid cash-only names.
       POSITIONAL   daily candles, the WHOLE market, found once a
                    day after the close. Free - it reads the
                    cache the desk already keeps.

   EQUITY AND FUTURES, ONE CALL
       Every sweep is flashed ONCE, for the stock. Where the
       stock also has a futures contract the call carries the lot
       size, the expiry, and what one lot costs at that price.
       A stock future tracks its own share price, so the sweep is
       the SAME sweep on both charts - scanning NFO separately
       would only produce a duplicate of every call.
       Three buttons filter the list: EVERYTHING / CAN TRADE IN
       FUTURES / EQUITY ONLY.

   THE CHART
       Click any row and the chart opens underneath it: the forty
       candles leading up to the sweep, with the level that was
       swept (dashed amber), the buy price (blue), the stop
       (red), the target (green), and the sweep bar itself marked.
       Drawn from the SAME exchange candles the sweep was found
       in - the bars Zerodha sends the desk - so the picture and
       the row can never disagree. A link to the full TradingView
       chart sits under every one.

   WHAT THE HISTORY SAYS  -  read this before trusting it
       INTRADAY, 718,512 sweeps tested over a year of 300 stocks:
           every sweep                    -0.024% before costs
           + reversal candle              -0.012%
           + previous day's extreme swept +0.006%   <- best
           This is the ONLY structure tested all week that PASSED
           a permutation test. The effect is real. It is also
           about a twelfth of the size needed to cover brokerage,
           so after costs it still loses ~0.077% a trade.

       POSITIONAL, 123,541 daily sweeps over three years:
           loses at every target from 0.75R to 2R, on both halves
           of the data, BEFORE costs. The reason is structural: a
           daily sweep bar is big, so the stop sits about 4.25%
           away and the entry is at that bar's high. Wide risk,
           poor price.

       Both are shown anyway. One year is not a verdict, and
       recording costs nothing.
