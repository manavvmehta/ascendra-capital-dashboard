   BEFORE 9:15 - FOUR WINDOWS, IN THIS ORDER
   -----------------------------------------

   1.  Double-click        ASCENDRA.py          (in 5 LIVE FEED)

   2.  Press  1            log in to Zerodha

          - your browser opens the Zerodha login
          - log in as normal
          - it lands on a page that FAILS TO LOAD. That is
            CORRECT. Nothing has gone wrong.
          - copy the WHOLE address from the address bar
          - paste it into the black window, press Enter
          - it shows live prices. That is the feed working.

       Do this FIRST. Everything below uses this one login.

   3.  Press Enter, then press  2        starts the desk

          - your dashboard opens at localhost:5055

   4.  Double-click        FORMATIONS LIVE.bat     (Patterns)

   5.  Double-click        SMC TWO BOOKS.bat       (SMC short + long)

   6.  Double-click        COMMODITIES LIVE.bat    (Gold, Silver,
                                                    Crude, Copper)

   7.  LEAVE ALL FOUR BLACK WINDOWS OPEN.

       Each window IS an engine. Close one and that tab stops
       being watched - and a stop nobody is watching is not a
       stop.


   WHAT EACH WINDOW IS DOING
   -------------------------

   ASCENDRA.py          serves the dashboard on localhost:5055

   FORMATIONS LIVE      the Patterns book - scans for formations
                        rated 8 and above

   SMC TWO BOOKS        the two SMC books, short term and long
                        term, AND the live price for the whole
                        desk - it quotes every stock once a
                        minute, so if this window is closed every
                        tab's PRICE NOW goes stale

   COMMODITIES LIVE     Gold, Silver, Crude and Copper on the
                        daily chart


   THEN NOTHING
   ------------

   The dashboard re-reads itself every 30 seconds, so prices move
   and new calls appear on their own. You do not need to refresh.

   The bell at the bottom right flashes a new call and keeps a
   list of the day's calls - left-click it to see them, and
   clicking a line takes you to that engine.

   Look at the dashboard whenever you like: localhost:5055

       TODAY             what to place, what you hold, what closed
       OPEN POSITIONS    everything you are carrying, all books
       the engine tabs   Live, Waiting, Booked, in that order


   AT THE END OF THE DAY
   ---------------------

   Close the windows. Everything is already saved.


================================================================
   IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
================================================================

   "Today's login is missing"
        You started an engine before logging in. Do step 2 first,
        then start that engine again.

   Dashboard will not load
        The ASCENDRA.py window is closed. Start it again.

   A tab says the engine is not running
        That engine's black window is closed. Start its .bat
        again. The tab says which one.

   PRICE NOW looks stale on every tab
        SMC TWO BOOKS is closed or was never started. That is the
        window that quotes prices for the whole desk.

   No calls appearing
        Normal for a quiet morning. The engines are selective and
        only fire when a setup genuinely appears. Check the
        WAITING group on each tab: that shows what they are
        watching, so you can tell "nothing happening" from
        "nothing firing".

   Anything else
        Screenshot the black window and send it to Claude.
        Do not try to fix it - several of the warnings that
        appear are completely normal, and only the full text
        says which.


================================================================
   THE ONE THING TO REMEMBER

   Set your PC never to sleep on trading days. If it sleeps
   mid-session the engines go quiet without saying so, and you
   lose the day's record.
