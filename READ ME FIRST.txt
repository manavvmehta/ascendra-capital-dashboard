  THE ONLY FILE YOU OPEN
  ----------------------

        5 LIVE FEED  ->  ASCENDRA.py

  Double-click it. It shows where things stand and asks what you want to do.
  Everything else is run from its menu.


  YOUR DAILY ROUTINE
  ------------------

  Before 9:15       open ASCENDRA.py, press 1  (log in to Zerodha)
                    then press 2               (start the desk)

  Leave that window open all day. It IS the engine. Close it and open calls
  stop being watched - and a stop nobody is watching is not a stop.

  Your dashboard is at http://localhost:5055 and refreshes itself.

  After the close   press 3 if you want tonight's swing calls written up.


  WHAT THE MENU DOES
  ------------------

  1  LOG IN TO ZERODHA
     Twenty seconds. Zerodha's access expires at the end of every day - that
     is their rule, and every system built on Kite does this same login.

  2  START THE DESK
     Runs the call engine against live prices and serves your website.
     Scans the whole market every 150 seconds; re-checks any open call every
     5 seconds.

  3  SCAN NOW
     After the close. Runs the swing setup over the day's final prices and
     writes any calls it finds, with entry, stop and both targets.

  4  BACKTEST
     One-off, about twenty minutes. Replays the same rules over three years
     of real prices and reports what they would actually have made or lost.
     Results appear on the BACKTEST tab of your dashboard.


  THE FOLDERS
  -----------

  5 LIVE FEED     everything you run, plus your Zerodha keys.
                  KEEP kite_keys.txt PRIVATE - anyone holding your API
                  secret can act on your trading account.

  3 CALL ENGINE   the engine itself, written by Jenil. The five setups, the
                  scoring, the lifecycle of a call. HOW_IT_WORKS.txt in there
                  explains every rule in plain English - worth reading, since
                  it is what you are trusting with money.

  WEBSITE         the dashboard page the desk serves.

  DATA            everything the system has produced. calls_log.csv is your
                  permanent record and survives restarts - that file is the
                  reason you will eventually know whether this works.

  1 DOWNLOAD DATA  NOT CURRENTLY USED. Ten years of NSE end-of-day prices,
                  from before the Zerodha feed existed. Kite now supplies
                  everything the engine needs. Kept only because it includes
                  companies that have since delisted, which Kite does not -
                  that matters for an honest long backtest one day. Safe to
                  delete if you want the disk space back.

  2 ENGINE        PARKED. The inverted head-and-shoulders work, from before
  4 REFERENCE     we changed direction. Nothing runs it. Kept in case you
                  come back to that pattern.
