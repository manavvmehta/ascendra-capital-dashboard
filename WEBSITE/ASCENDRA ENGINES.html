1 · SMC short term

15-minute chart, cash market, long only, squared off the same day.

The sweepPrice runs through yesterday's high or low and closes back the other side inside one 15-minute candle. That candle is the sweep. No candle shape is required — the shape is named on the card but decides nothing.
StopThe sweep candle's low. It must sit 1.5%–3% below that candle's high, measured on the trigger. Outside the band the call is not written.
TriggerPrice must then close above the sweep candle's high. That is what says the sweep was real.
EntryYou do not chase the break. Wait for price to come back into the sweep candle's own body and buy there. Entry = the body low.
TargetTwice the risk, measured entry to stop.
ExitOut if a candle CLOSES below the stop — not if it merely touches. Otherwise squared off on the 15:15 candle.
Fill windowMust fill the same day it breaks out, or the call is given up.
Cost0.059% round trip, intraday cash.
New todayNo call is written from the 15:00 candle onward, and no call may become an order after 15:00. I added this at your instruction; it loads on restart.

Patterns detectedTen: ascending, descending and symmetrical triangle; double bottom and double top; head-and-shoulders and its inverse; cup-and-handle; bull flag and bear flag. Symmetrical triangle is long only.
Two sectionsSWING finds the pattern on the daily chart and enters on the hourly. INTRADAY finds it on the hourly and enters on 15-minute. The desk renames them: the daily one is your POSITIONAL pot, the hourly one your SWING pot. INTRADAY is F&O names only; any short call is F&O only.

Two things I found in the code that you should know before trusting the tested figures.

1. The live target may not be the tested target. The file keeps a table of per-pattern target fractions described as “lifted from the backtest, unchanged” — 0.73 of the pattern height for a double bottom, 0.46 for a flag, and so on. But the function that sets the live target divides the target by that same fraction, which reconstructs the full pattern height. So the live book reaches for the whole measured move where the table records a fraction of it. If the backtest used the fraction, every live target is 1.4–2× too far away and the win rate cannot match. I cannot tell from your files which the backtest used.

2. There is no holding period. No time exit, no maximum hold, no end-of-day square-off anywhere in the file. A position closes only on its stop or its target. Anything that hits neither stays open indefinitely.

3. The record was corrupted until 23/09. The file states 165 of 191 rows had been settled from candles that printed before the call existed, and says plainly that any average computed from it is fiction. Only calls first seen after that fix are worth anything.

4 · Commodities

Daily candles, long only, four MCX contracts.

Entry ruleAll of: the day closes above its 20-day high (the highest high of the previous 20 days, strictly greater); TRIX positive (18-period triple EMA of closes); weekly Choppiness under 50 (14-week); and a strong breakout candle — body at least 70% of the day's range and green.
Entry priceThe next day's open. Nothing is assumed — the call is written with the entry blank and filled in when tomorrow opens. If that open is already below the stop the call is voided, no trade.
StopThe 20-day low, fixed at signal time. Never trailed.
TargetTwice the risk. A gap through either level is honoured at the open, not at the level.
Give upAfter 40 bars held, out at that day's close.
UniverseFour: GOLD (lot 100), SILVER (30), CRUDEOIL (100), COPPER (2500). Cut from sixteen on 28/09 at your request, because the mini and micro contracts are the same commodity in a smaller lot and were producing three calls on one move.

Three code-level notes.
